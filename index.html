Best way forward

An open rollout we can actually ship.

The fastest route is to prove one focused track at a time. Start with AI-led trading, learn from real performance data, then expand into AI-led media, consulting, entertainment, gaming, and social with the right safety foundations.

Phase 01 · Now

Investment + trading dashboard

Use NYUKD.com to introduce the portfolio, then start with AI-led trading: strategy scope, risk rules, and a dashboard for real performance reporting.

Phase 02 · Next

Infotainment pilot

Publish a small series of AI-led explainers with human review, visible sources, and a repeatable editorial workflow.

Phase 03

Consulting offer

Turn process transformation and operational AI into a clear consulting offer: diagnostics, operating model, roadmap, and implementation support.

Phase 04

Entertainment + Gaming pilots

Test one AI-led story or video and one playable title before scaling creative output or building a larger catalogue.

Phase 05

Social MVP + launch ops

Build Social only after trust, safety, verification, consent, analytics, and support operations are ready.
