Practice 03 . Adaptive Operating Model

Evolve your processes.
Adapt your products.
With AI on every cycle.

An adaptive operating model for AI-led evolution of processes, products and operating routines. Five disciplined motions. A managed artefact set your steering committee will recognise. AI integrated into every cycle. Proven across eight live engagements.

Motion 01 Diagnose Assess the current state. Frame the smallest evidence-producing hypothesis with an explicit success condition.
Motion 02 Design Model the target-state slice and its AI components. Reuse where reuse exists; engineer where it does not.
Motion 03 Pilot Operate the slice end-to-end against the success condition — by inspection, smoke test or instrumented capture.
Motion 04 Embed Integrate into the live process or product. Production gates enforced; no parallel staging unless governance requires it.
Motion 05 Evolve Verify in production. Each finding becomes the next slice; each pivot becomes the next direction.
