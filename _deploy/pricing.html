Common questions

Pricing FAQ

Do I need a broker account to start?

No. Free and Pro both run on a managed paper account that we provide — real fills, real timestamps, no real money. You only need your own broker (Alpaca, eToro, or Interactive Brokers) when you upgrade to Premium and decide to go live.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account's Subscription page or through the Stripe billing portal. Access continues until the end of the current billing period, then drops back to Free.

What's a "strategy slot"?

A strategy slot is one running instance of a trading strategy — either one of our four defaults (Momentum, Unsharp, Box, Pullback) or your own logic plugged in via the Strategy Builder. Free includes 1 slot, Pro 2, Premium 4.

How is FOUNDER50 applied?

Enter the code FOUNDER50 at checkout to get 50% off any paid plan for 24 months. Available to the first 50 sign-ups while we're in live beta. Standard pricing resumes automatically after 24 months.

Is NYUKD Invest a financial advisor?

No. NYUKD Invest is infrastructure for automated day trading — we provide tools to make disciplined trading easier. We do not give investment advice, signals, or recommendations. Please read our T&Cs before subscribing.
