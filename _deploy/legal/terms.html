The one-line summary. NYUKD Invest is software infrastructure for automated day trading. We are not a financial advisor, a signal service, or a guarantor of returns. You decide what to trade, when to go live, and what risk to take.

1. What NYUKD Invest is

NYUKD Invest is a software platform that lets you plug a trading strategy (your own or one of our defaults) into an automation slot. The platform books trades, manages stop-losses, and closes positions on your behalf according to the rules of the strategy you've selected.

By default, your slots run on a managed paper-trading account that simulates real fills using delayed market data. You only place real money at risk when you explicitly connect your own broker account and switch your slots from Paper to Live.

2. What NYUKD Invest is not

  • Not a financial advisor. We don't provide investment, tax, or legal advice. Any signals, picks, or strategy outputs are produced by software running on rules — they are not recommendations.
  • Not a custodian. When you go live, trades happen in your own broker account (Alpaca, eToro, Interactive Brokers, or others we support). We never hold your funds, and you can revoke our API access at any time from your broker's settings.
  • Not a signal service. The Stock Picker ranks tickers using public-data heuristics. We don't sell, syndicate, or promise the accuracy of these ranks.

5. Risks

Day trading carries substantial risk of loss. Strategy performance on paper trading does not guarantee live trading performance. Slippage, broker latency, partial fills, and market gaps will all affect your real-money results. The historical performance of any default strategy is provided for context and does not predict future returns.

You should only allocate capital you can afford to lose. You're responsible for monitoring your slots, setting your own risk limits, and disabling automation if something looks wrong.

6. Bring-your-own-credentials (BYOC)

When you connect a broker on the Premium plan, NYUKD Invest stores your API credentials encrypted and uses them only to place trades on your behalf according to the strategy you've selected. We never use your credentials for any other purpose. You can revoke access at any time from your broker's API settings; once revoked, our access stops immediately.
